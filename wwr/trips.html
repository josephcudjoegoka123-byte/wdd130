Beginner - Family Float

Perfect for first-timers and families with kids! Enjoy a calm and scenic 2-hour float down the lower section of the river. Our expert guides will make sure everyone is safe and having fun. No experience needed.

  • Duration: 2 Hours
  • Difficulty: Class I-II (Easy)
  • Age Limit: 6+ years
  • Includes: Gear, guide, snacks

Intermediate - Canyon Adventure

Get ready for excitement! This half-day trip takes you through stunning canyons and thrilling Class III rapids. Ideal for those who have rafted before and want more action. Lunch included on the riverbank.

  • Duration: 4 Hours (Half Day)
  • Difficulty: Class III (Moderate)
  • Age Limit: 12+ years
  • Includes: Gear, guide, lunch, photos

Advanced - Extreme Rapids Challenge

The ultimate adrenaline rush! Conquer our most challenging Class IV-V rapids on this full-day expedition. Steep drops, huge waves, and non-stop action. For experienced and physically fit rafters only.

  • Duration: 7 Hours (Full Day)
  • Difficulty: Class IV-V (Hard)
  • Age Limit: 16+ years
  • Includes: Premium gear, expert guide, lunch, dinner
